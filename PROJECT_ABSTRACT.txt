PROJECT TITLE: TRUTHLENS AI - FAKE NEWS DETECTION SYSTEM

ABSTRACT

In the digital era, misinformation spreads across social media and news platforms at
unprecedented speed, eroding public trust and enabling harmful consequences ranging
from public panic to electoral manipulation. Detecting and categorizing fake news has
therefore become an urgent and essential challenge for individuals, journalists, and
platform providers alike.

TruthLens AI is proposed as a robust and explainable fake-news detection system that
combines the power of deep learning with rigorous, evidence-based verification. At its
core lies a trained neural network — an Embedding layer feeding a Bidirectional Gated
Recurrent Unit (BiGRU) — which reads the submitted news article and produces an
initial classification of REAL or FAKE along with confidence probabilities. Because a
purely statistical classifier alone is not sufficient to earn trust, TruthLens AI
augments this prediction with a comprehensive verification engine that decomposes the
article into atomic claims, cross-references each claim against a curated knowledge
base and live news sources retrieved from free keyless RSS feeds (Google News,
Bing News, and DuckDuckGo), scores relevance, tiers the credibility of sources, and
finally issues an evidence-based verdict of REAL, FALSE, or UNVERIFIED. A free
cloud-based AI judge — chained across Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, ChatGPT, and Ollama —
reviews the assembled evidence and delivers a final verdict that is always grounded in
the retrieved sources and is never fabricated when no provider can decide.

The system is engineered as a production-ready web application: a Python Flask backend
serves a comprehensive REST API, while a responsive React 18 frontend built with Vite,
TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Recharts presents the classification results,
explainability data, evidence matrices, and model-performance dashboards in a clean,
user-friendly interface. The architecture is fully modular and deployable on Vercel as
a serverless function, providing real-time fact-checking with a high-confidence final
verdict, per-claim transparency with retrieved citations, an honest UNVERIFIED
response when evidence is insufficient, and graceful offline operation that never
relies on paid API keys.

TruthLens AI demonstrates a practical and trustworthy approach to automated
misinformation detection, pairing neural classification with transparent,
source-backed reasoning to help users identify falsehoods quickly and confidently.
